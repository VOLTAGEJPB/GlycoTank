GlycoTank

Welcome to GlycoTank

Glycogen is carbohydrate stored in your muscles and liver: your short-term fuel.

  • It refills when you eat carbs and drains when you train.
  • A low tank often shows up as low energy, brain fog or carb cravings.
  • It's fuel, not body fat, and it changes day to day.

Why track your tank?

  • Smarter carbs: eat more around hard sessions and less on rest days.
  • Better sessions: avoid training hard on an empty tank when quality matters.
  • Easier dieting: expect more hunger on low-tank days and lean on protein and fiber.

How it works

  • Meals add about 85% of their carbs to the tank.
  • Your body uses some every day, plus more for training based on your sets, cardio minutes and intensity.
  • The carb planner suggests how much to eat for tomorrow's training.
  • No account, no servers: your data stays on your phone.

GlycoTank gives general fitness estimates, not medical advice.
